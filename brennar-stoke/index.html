Brennar Stoke

A former Jedi who runs cargo on the Outer Rim under the name Stoker.

Contents BiographyThe Kreel incidentPersonalityEquipmentThe EmberBehind the scenes

Brennar Stoke was a Jedi of Luke Skywalker's academy who left the Order before its destruction and spent the following years flying cargo on the Outer Rim. Under the alias Stoker he runs spice routes that double as supply lines for the Resistance.

He still carries the blue lightsaber he built as a student, and he uses the Force rarely.

"They wanted to survive. I wanted to do what we were there to do."Stoke, on why he left the Jedi

Biography

The academy

Stoke came to the Jedi as an adult, which was unusual. He had already lived a life by then, and he joined because he believed the Order existed to stand between the galaxy and the things that hurt it.

He was never the strongest student, but he was disciplined and he asked hard questions. He built his lightsaber around a blue kyber crystal.

Leaving the Order

Stoke was among the students who noticed Ben Solo coming apart, and one of the few who said so out loud. He went to Master Skywalker more than once to ask what the Order intended to do, for Ben and for itself. The answers he got were about patience and process, and about protecting what little remained of the Jedi after the Empire.

He came to believe the institution had made its own survival the point. He left without anger. He kept his saber, since the vow had been his and not the Order's, and he took the academy's old Consular-class transport with him. When the academy burned not long after, he grieved, but he was not surprised.

"I left when I realized 'patience' had become the name we gave to flinching."Stoke, to a cantina acquaintance

Smuggler

Under the name Stoker he began hauling cargo along the Outer Rim. Within a few years his spice runs were carrying Resistance medical supplies and comm relays under the manifest, and his ship had become a familiar sight at relay stations where nobody asked many questions.

The Kreel incident

Three years before the current bounty on his head, Stoke disarmed a young First Order lieutenant during a raid on a supply drop and let him walk away. He has said since that he would do it again.

Three months later that lieutenant, by then Captain Tavor Kreel of the First Order Security Bureau, led the raid on Nadiri that killed eleven people, among them the Resistance operative Maela Vance. Stoke does not talk about Nadiri with strangers. When pressed by someone he trusts, he tells the whole thing and ends it the same way every time.

"I'd do it again. That's the part I can't talk my way out of."Stoke, on Kreel

Kreel has since posted a 40,000 credit bounty for Stoke, to be taken alive.

Personality and traits

Stoke is quiet and dry. He answers questions about his past with one of two rehearsed lines, a blunt one for strangers and a wry one for people he has decided to talk to, and he rarely goes further than that unless someone pushes.

A few habits of speech followed him out of the academy:

  • "Tell me when." What he says instead of saying he is ready.
  • "Both at once." How he ends an argument he does not intend to concede, usually when two true things refuse to fit together.
  • "Walk light." What he says instead of goodbye, and only to people he cares about. Dock workers who have known him for years have never heard it.

The alias follows an old Jedi habit. Obi-Wan Kenobi spent nineteen years as Ben, and Stoke did the same thing to his own name, keeping the sounds and dropping syllables so it would still turn his head when someone called it across a room.

Equipment

Stoke's lightsaber is the one he built at the academy. The blade is still blue. He has never bled the crystal or replaced it, and that bothers him more than he lets on. He carries it under his vest, where a hooded poncho-cape hides the line of it.

In the Ember's cabin he keeps a shelf of five objects, one from each of five jobs: a cracked pocket watch, a scorched First Order rank cylinder, a child's tooka doll with one ear missing, a pressed flower of a species he has never been able to identify, and a Rebel starbird pendant on a frayed cord.

The Ember

The Ember is a heavily modified Old Republic Consular-class cruiser, the same diplomatic hull once used by Jedi envoys. It looks like a wreck from the outside. Everything that matters inside is in perfect order.

The name on the hull is newer than the scorch marks around it. Behind the pilot's seat, half burned, the maker's plaque still carries the ship's registered name, The Last Light. It was the academy's last transport, and the only ship that got out.

Behind the scenes

Brennar Stoke is an original character created by Brandon Stoker of Stoken Games for Halcy-Con 2026, a scenario-based Star Wars convention where attendees play original characters set after The Force Awakens.
