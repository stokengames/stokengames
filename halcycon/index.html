This takes about ten minutes, and it goes faster with a second pair of eyes.

Evidence 1 · Intercepted transmission

Relay 7 caught the tail end of a call from the smuggler to a Resistance frequency, sent in the old dock tap code Republic crews used before the war. Read each pair as row first, then column. I and J share a cell.

From the file: at the academy, Stoke was known for never saying goodbye. He had his own way of ending a conversation, and the younger students picked it up from him.

The first pair, 52, is row 5 and column 2, which gives you W. Work through the other eight the same way. With two people it goes quicker if one reads the pairs out loud while the other finds the letters.

Evidence 2 · The vessel

When Stoke left the academy he took a Consular-class cruiser with him, an old Republic diplomatic hull that nobody has seen since. Four ships are docked at relay 7 tonight, and the dock master's log is too much of a mess to say which is which, but his harbor notes fill in enough to work it out.

Ships: Ember, Gilded Sow, Tessek's Fortune, Vantage.
Hulls: Consular-class cruiser, YT light freighter, Ghtroc freighter, Lambda shuttle.

  1. The Gilded Sow is a YT light freighter and sits in bay 1.
  2. The Lambda shuttle is in bay 4. Its crew paid docking fees in First Order scrip.
  3. The Consular-class hull is docked directly beside the Gilded Sow.
  4. Vantage is not in bay 2 and not in bay 4.
  5. Tessek's Fortune's crew also paid in First Order scrip.
The third note puts the Consular in bay 2, so the question is which ships can be ruled out of bay 2 and which one is left.

Bay 2 holds a ship with EMBER painted on the hull in letters newer than the scorch marks around them. On a Consular, the name that counts is the one on the maker's plaque behind the pilot's seat, and this one has been through a fire.

THE   LAST   LIGHT
CONSULAR-CLASS · KEEL LAID CORUSCANT

From the file: the academy's last transport was never recovered. The few who got out gave it a name that night, and the name stuck.

Five letters are burned out of twelve, across three words. Think about what you would name the only ship that made it out of a burning temple.
